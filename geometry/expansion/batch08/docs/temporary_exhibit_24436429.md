# Temporary Exhibit: historical identity found, current form unresolved

Research date: 2026-09-13. Recommendation: defer this target pending current evidence. After review, the coordinator authorized moving the intact feature to `../deferred/` and removing it from the active manifest. Original source asset and global completion state remain unchanged.

The mapped 173.651 m² rectangle is on the Natural History Museum front grounds. A strong historical match is the Sensational Butterflies enclosure: the depicted-object coordinate in [Geograph photo 4561180 / Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sensational_Butterflies_-_geograph.org.uk_-_4561180.jpg), 51.496177,-0.174631, projects inside the complete target polygon (distance zero). Geotags are not a survey, and an exact perimeter match remains unverified.

The photograph was actually viewed. It shows a low, single-volume white arched enclosure with repeated roof ribs, opaque lower panels and a ticket entrance. There is no four-storey masonry facade. Its appearance strongly contradicts using the old 13.15 m/four-floor typology as a reconstruction of that historical exhibit. A deliberately broad historical roof-apex estimate is 3–7 m based on doorway and adult-visitor scale; perspective and grade are uncalibrated, so this is only an order-of-magnitude visual estimate, not a measured height or a current model contract. Materials, hidden rear face and exact arch section are unknown.

Photo attribution: **Sensational Butterflies, Oast House Archive, 7 July 2015, CC BY-SA 2.0**, [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). Original JPEG and Commons metadata are saved in `../references/temporary_exhibit_24436429/`; no photo texture has been exported. The evidence ledger records source, hash, viewpoint and permitted uses.

The [Natural History Museum's official grounds history](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/about-us/a-history-of-the-museum-grounds-and-wildlife-garden.html) states that transformed gardens opened on 18 July 2024, linking the east and west grounds. It describes the new Evolution Garden and its geological/biological timeline. This supports a material temporal mismatch risk, but does not explicitly document the removal date of this tent or the present condition of this exact rectangle. Only factual text was used; no copyrighted site images were acquired or derived.

Eight bounded searches yielded one useful licensed exterior photo. No recent licensed photograph of this exact footprint was identified. Consequently current shape and current height remain unknown. A historic tent must not silently replace a purported present-day building; nor should its old generated entrance or four-floor facade be treated as measured facts. Next useful evidence would be a dated current licensed view of this garden patch, or an explicit choice to reconstruct the 2015 exhibition as a historical layer.
