## Height interpretation

Official wording is only “thirty metre tower”: no explicit ground datum, roof-versus-balustrade endpoint or precision is supplied. Treat it as rounded architectural height. A broad same-east-return comparison in the oblique photo places maincornice at roughly.65–.78 of the visible tower height. Vehicle occlusion and perspective prevent precise ratios; nearer north-front verticals cannot be treated as coplanar with the farther tower.

Conditionally using30m as an approximate reference gives maincornice about20–23.5m and overall mansard roof about22.5–26m. The coordinator selected **21.5m mainwall plus2.5m attic** for778and777, explicitly visual estimates. Original16.3m derived from3.15m/floor is retained as baseline history but would make a30m tower disproportionately dominant. Each five-storey facade must be recomposed at revised floor heights; do not stretch only its top storey. Absolute surfaces use eachfeaturebase+relativeheight. Roof maximum is not a shared-wall height.
