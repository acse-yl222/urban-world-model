## Scope limits

Source OSM footprints are retained; inherited wall heights remain estimates. Jamaican High Commission has identifiable architectural photo evidence, with a deliberately simplified ground-level entrance instead of a reconstructed historic terrace. 29 is a revised, largely inferred northern wing. Jay Mews and 84 Kensington Gore lack confirmed target elevations; their bay dimensions, roof interpretation and unseen details are artistic completion. No measured dimensions, basement reconstruction or geographic accuracy certificate is claimed.

Google imagery was not digitized or exported. One Geograph download for 84 returned403 and was stopped; it was not used. The diagnostic neighborhood keeps source scalar PBR colors and omits source image textures. The live viewer retains the original source city textures.
