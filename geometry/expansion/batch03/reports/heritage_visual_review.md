## Remaining uncertainty

The compared reference is Robin Sones's 2020 Geograph 6577546 south-facade photograph, supported by Historic England 1211833 text. Exact metric dimensions, brick carvings, facade projections, roof intersections, chimney forms and hidden elevations are interpreted. The inherited scene eaves and ground-level doorway were retained; the photographed raised historic terrace and basement were deliberately not reconstructed. The 29 northern-wing roof and rear windows remain artistic completions, and the southern photograph is not direct evidence for them. Procedural colors and simplified ornament do not reproduce the full historic facade detail.
